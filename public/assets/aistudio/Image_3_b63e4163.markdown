## Brand & Style

The design system embodies deep focus, hyper-precision, and latent computational intelligence. Crafted specifically for dense AI-driven document orchestration, contextual search, and high-throughput Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) workspaces, it strips away cognitive friction while framing synthetic reasoning in an elite, obsidian canvas.

### Visual Style
- **Cyber-Glassmorphism meets Linear Utility**: Deep black and indigo-tinted obsidian foundations layered with crisp 1px structural glass borders, subtle inner radiance, and ambient electric purple backlights.
- **Atmospheric Depth**: Controlled luminescence using soft, focused diffusion rings (`rgba(108, 99, 255, 0.15)` to `0.35`) behind active model outputs, vector chunk visualizations, and synthesis streams.
- **Information Density**: Compact horizontal alignments, hairline separators, and micro-metric badges tuned for technical operators, legal teams, researchers, and data scientists parsing dense reference corpora.

## Colors

The palette operates strictly in dark mode, balancing an absolute dark abyss with high-vibrancy electrical accents to guide visual hierarchy across multi-document workflows.

### Color Tokens & Semantics
- **Background Root (`#0a0a0f`)**: Deep void canvas. Used for the viewport base, outer application shell, and primary reading plane.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#111118`)**: Grounding surface for persistent sidebars, top utility headers, and contextual inspection panels.
- **Surface Level 2 / Card (`#16161f`)**: Glass-backed layer for document tiles, interactive chat cells, citation inspector drawers, and vector payload cards.
- **Border Subtle (`#2a2a3a`)**: Low-contrast hairline dividers (`1px solid`) providing edge separation across dark fields without visual noise.
- **Primary / Electric Purple (`#6c63ff`)**: System ignition color. Used for active generation indicators, focal CTAs, neural pathway nodes, and focus rings with a signature glow (`rgba(108, 99, 255, 0.3)`).
